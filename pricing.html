Frequently Asked Questions

Is the first 3 months really free for lecturers?

Yes. During the official launch period, lecturers get 3 months free access before subscription billing starts.

When will I be charged after the free period?

After the launch promo ends, new users receive 1 month free trial before billing starts.

Can students switch from per submission to semester access?

Yes. Students can upgrade to semester access from their dashboard when billing is connected.

Can I use semester access across multiple courses?

Yes. Semester access is designed for all registered courses linked to the student account.

How do I get my referral code?

Lecturers receive a unique referral code after creating a lecturer account and activating their plan.

What payment methods are available?

The payment system will support card, bank transfer, USSD, and other local payment methods through backend integration.
